# Case Study — Fable LMS iOS App

**Intern:** Nishant Sinha
**Role:** Scrum Master & iOS Frontend Developer
**Organisation:** Infosys, India
**Duration:** June – July 2024

---

## Context

Built during a 2-month iOS Developer internship at Infosys as part of a mock SaaS client engagement. The goal was to design and ship a fully functional Library Management System — branded as **Fable** — across two iOS apps: one for end users, one for librarians and admins.

---

## The Challenge

- Two apps sharing one Firebase backend with role-based permissions
- Real-time data sync across devices for both apps
- Ship both apps within a 2-month internship window
- Manage a 10-person cross-functional team using Agile/Scrum

---

## My Contributions

### Frontend (SwiftUI)
Built the complete UI layer for both apps — every screen, every flow:
- All authentication flows (login, signup, role-based routing)
- Home screens, catalog browsers, progress dashboards
- Monthly analytics dashboard with stats cards, bar charts, donut charts
- Requests & approvals flow with Pending/Approved tab switching
- Campaign management screens
- Book upload flow with success states
- User profile and borrowing history

### Scrum Master
- Ran daily standups and weekly sprint planning
- Managed Jira board, tracked and resolved 10+ sprint issues
- Coordinated between UI/UX, backend, and team lead

### Accessibility
- Proposed and implemented adaptive color scheme for system invert mode
- Improves usability for color-blind users — adopted in the final build

---

## Outcome

- Both apps (User + Admin/Librarian) shipped on schedule
- Clean separation of roles across User, Librarian, and Admin personas
- Live Firebase sync working across all iOS devices in demo
- App branded and presented as "Fable" to the mock SaaS client
